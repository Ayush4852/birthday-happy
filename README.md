## ▶️ How to view it

**Option 1 — Open locally**
Just double-click `index.html` — it opens in any browser.
(Keep all 4 items together in the same folder.)

**Option 2 — Host online for free (recommended for sharing)**
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the entire `birthday-site` folder onto the page
3. You get a public link in 5 seconds — send it to her 💌

## ✨ What's inside

- Custom **Barbie doll** SVG waving with a sparkle wand in the hero
- **Love-o-meter** that fills as she taps things (max it out for a surprise!)
- **Scroll progress bar** at the top
- Floating **parallax** background shapes
- **Pop the balloons** mini-game — 8 hidden messages
- **Open the gift** — animated 3D-looking gift box
- **Photo gallery** — swipe, 3D tilt on touch, tap to enlarge
- **Interactive cake** — blow each candle out
- **Play Happy Birthday** melody (real audio, no files needed)
- **Wishes jar** — tap to draw a folded wish from 15 unique ones
- **8 flip cards** of reasons I love you
- **Send a kiss** — flies up the screen with sparkle trail
- **Heart trail** follows finger drags
- **Soft sound feedback** on every tap
- **Secret easter egg** — tap "Akanksha" 5 times in the hero 🤫

## ✏️ To personalize

Open `index.html` in any text editor and search for:
- `Ayush` → your name
- `My dearest Akku` → the love letter
- `Akku, if I could wrap` → the gift box message

Open `script.js` and search for:
- `balloonMsgs` → the 8 balloon-pop messages
- `wishes` → the 15 wishes-jar wishes
- `HB` → the Happy Birthday melody (note durations)

That's it. Made with 💖.
